# 🐉 DRAGON'S HOARD

A hoard-stealing card duel against Vermathrax, the Hoard-Keeper — an entry for
make.fun's **best card game** bounty.

You are a thief in a dragon's lair. **Strike** the beast down, or **Pilfer** its
golden hoard and spend the gold to **upgrade** your cards. Every theft feeds the
dragon's **Rage** — at full rage it telegraphs a huge **Dragon Breath** attack you
must Guard against. At 20 Life the dragon enrages; at 10 Life it guards its hoard,
and only a big Strike breaks the guard so you can steal again.

- 30 Life each side, draw 2 per turn, gain Ember to play cards
- 5 card types told apart by colour **and** shape: Strike (red sword), Pilfer
  (gold coin), Guard (blue shield), Mend (green leaf), Trick (purple star)
- The dragon's next move is telegraphed a full turn ahead — read it and counter it
- Title, table, win and loss screens; keyboard + mouse; WebAudio bleeps in code

Play it: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or run `node serve.ts`
and visit http://localhost:8080.
