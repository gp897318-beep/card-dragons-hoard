🐉 DRAGON'S HOARD

A hoard-stealing card duel against Vermathrax, the Hoard-Keeper

The dragon sleeps on a mountain of gold. Strike it down — or Pilfer its hoard, spend the gold to upgrade your cards, and survive its Rage. At 20 Life it enrages. At 10 Life it guards its hoard — break the guard with a big Strike before you can steal again.
⚔️ STRIKE (red sword) — damage the dragon  •  🪙 PILFER (gold coin) — steal gold, feeds Rage
🛡️ GUARD (blue shield) — block the next hit  •  🍃 MEND (green leaf) — heal
⭐ TRICK (purple star) — soothe rage, feint, kindle Ember  •  ⬢ EMBER — pay to play a card
🏆 First to reduce the rival to 0 Life wins. Both start at 30 Life.
